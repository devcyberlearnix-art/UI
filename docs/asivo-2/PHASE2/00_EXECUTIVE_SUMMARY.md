3. **Forensic Route Inventory:** Debunking previous estimates of 54 routes to formally map, categorize, and validate all **100 declared routes** in `src/App.jsx`.
4. **Backend Security & Privacy Realism:** Honestly reporting the status of backend microservices, establishing FERPA/COPPA compliance realities, and formally classifying the Parent Observatory backend as blocked/staged rather than making unfounded claims.
5. **Rigorous Quality Assurance & Performance:** Delivering automated E2E test evidence across 6 realistic journeys, achieving WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility, validating 7 responsive viewports, and generating 9 visual regression screenshots.
